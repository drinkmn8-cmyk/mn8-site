When life gets heavy

Travel happens. Stress spikes. Schedules break. Days get missed. MN8 is built for continuity under pressure. Missed days don’t reset progress, busy weeks don’t cancel momentum, and disruption doesn’t collapse the structure.

Integrity guarantee

MN8 does not guarantee speed, motivation, or a specific outcome timeline. MN8 guarantees the integrity of the structure when followed as designed.

If you complete 12 months inside MN8, follow it as designed, and there is no observable physical change compared to your documented baseline, your program investment will be refunded.
